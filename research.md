## Areas

## Doctoral research

**Urban air quality management using neuro-fuzzy modelling**
Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Madras · Advisor: Dr. S. M. Shiva Nagendra

- Statistical trend analysis of criteria pollutants and their relationship with meteorological parameters.
- Identification of the probability distribution of NAAQS violations, and prediction of violation frequency using extreme value theory.
- ANN-based forecasting of air quality from pollutant and meteorological data in Chennai.
- A fuzzy-logic AQI for air quality information and real-time health advisories.

## Open questions I am working on

1. How do long-term PM<sub>2.5</sub> records become robust exposure and life-expectancy metrics without hiding their uncertainty?
2. How can satellite, monitoring and population data be combined while keeping the chain of assumptions transparent?
3. Which emission sectors actually move the needle for city-level clean-air action?
4. Where does machine learning genuinely improve forecasting, calibration and spatial inference — and where does it just add opacity?
5. How should complex air pollution data be communicated to policymakers, journalists and the public?
