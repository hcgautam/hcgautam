# Research

My research and applied work focus on air pollution measurement, modeling, exposure assessment, and communication. The central goal is to make air quality data scientifically rigorous, reproducible, and useful for policy decisions.

## Ph.D. research

**Urban air quality management using neuro-fuzzy modeling**  
Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Madras  
Advisor: **Dr. S. M. Shiva Nagendra**

Core components of the Ph.D. work included:

- Statistical trend analysis of criteria pollutants and meteorological relationships.
- Probability distribution analysis of National Ambient Air Quality Standards violations.
- Use of extreme value theory for estimating violation frequency.
- ANN-based forecasting of air quality using pollutant and meteorological data in Chennai.
- Fuzzy logic-based AQI development for air quality information and real-time health advisory.

## Applied research questions

The following questions define the type of problems I work on:

1. How can long-term PM<sub>2.5</sub> datasets be converted into robust life-expectancy and exposure metrics?
2. How can satellite, monitoring, and population data be combined without losing transparency?
3. Which emission sectors matter most for city-level clean air action?
4. How well can machine learning improve forecasting, calibration, and spatial inference in air quality studies?
5. How should complex air pollution data be communicated to policy professionals, researchers, journalists, and the public?
