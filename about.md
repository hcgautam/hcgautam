# About

I am **Dr. Hrishikesh Chandra Gautam**, an air quality researcher with training in environmental engineering and experience across academic research, applied policy projects, data analytics, and machine learning for air pollution management.

My work focuses on converting complex air quality datasets into decision-ready evidence: satellite-derived PM<sub>2.5</sub>, monitoring observations, emission inventory data, low-cost sensor measurements, meteorology, population exposure, and life-expectancy metrics.

## Professional position

I currently work as a **Data Specialist - Air Quality Life Index** at the **Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago (EPIC)**. In this role, I work on the AQLI analysis and reporting pipeline, including satellite PM<sub>2.5</sub> processing, population-weighted exposure calculations, long-term trend analysis, visualization, and scientific communication.

## Research identity

My academic and applied work sits at the intersection of:

- Environmental engineering
- Urban air quality management
- Satellite-derived air pollution data
- Emission inventory development
- Statistical and machine learning models
- Air quality forecasting
- AQI and health-risk communication
- Policy-relevant visualization and reporting

Before joining EPIC, I worked at **CSTEP, Bengaluru** as a Senior Associate, contributing to emission inventories, air quality management plans, low-cost sensor data analysis, satellite-based PM<sub>2.5</sub> modeling, and reduced-complexity modeling efforts for Indian cities.

I have also worked at **IIT Madras** on projects related to vehicular emissions in Delhi and air quality management for the Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust region.
