I am **Dr. Hrishikesh Chandra Gautam**, an environmental engineer working on the measurement, modelling and communication of air pollution. My work sits where messy environmental data meets the decisions that have to be made from it: satellite PM<sub>2.5</sub>, ground monitoring, low-cost sensors, emission inventories, meteorology, population exposure and life-expectancy metrics.

I currently work as **Data Specialist — Air Quality Life Index** at the **Energy Policy Institute at the University of Chicago (EPIC)**, where I run the AQLI analysis and reporting pipeline: satellite PM<sub>2.5</sub> processing, population-weighted exposure calculation, long-term trend analysis across GADM Level-2 regions, visualisation, and the scientific communication that carries it into policy debate.

Before EPIC I spent nearly four years at **CSTEP, Bengaluru** as a Senior Associate, building emission inventories for 76 Indian cities, contributing to state and city clean-air action plans, evaluating low-cost sensors, modelling satellite-derived PM<sub>2.5</sub>, and contributing to PAVITRA, a reduced-complexity air pollution model for India. Earlier, at **IIT Madras**, I worked on vehicular emissions in Delhi (CADTIME) and air quality management for the Jawaharlal Nehru Port Trust region.

## How I work

I care about work that is reproducible and legible to the people who have to act on it. That means version-controlled analysis, transparent assumptions about where numbers come from, and charts that a journalist or a pollution control board officer can read without a methods section.
