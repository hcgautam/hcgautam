This website is a public home for my academic and professional work in air quality research, environmental engineering, and machine learning.

I plan to use this space for short notes on:

- air pollution data analysis,
- satellite-derived PM<sub>2.5</sub>,
- emission inventories,
- low-cost sensors,
- machine learning for air quality,
- air quality indices and health communication,
- policy-relevant visualizations and reports.

The goal is to make technical air quality work easier to understand, reproduce, and use for evidence-informed decisions.
